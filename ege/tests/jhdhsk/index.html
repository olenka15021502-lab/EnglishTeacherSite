Task 1 · Reading aloud

Imagine that you are preparing a project with your friend. You have found some interesting material for the presentation and you want to read this text to your friend. You have 1.5 minutes to read the text silently, then be ready to read it out aloud. You will not have more than 1.5 minutes to read it.

Crop circles are large patterns created by the flattening of crops such as wheat or barley. These intricate designs, often featuring geometric shapes, appear mysteriously overnight in fields, primarily in Southern England. While some believe they are the result of supernatural forces or extraterrestrial activity, the vast majority are known to be man-made hoaxes. The creation process, called “circle making,” involves teams of people using simple tools like planks and ropes to press down the crops in complex patterns. Despite the known human origins, the phenomenon continues to attract public fascination due to the scale, precision, and secrecy of the creations. The designs have become increasingly elaborate over the years, with some even encoding mathematic formulas. While they cause temporary damage to the crops, they also bring tourists and generate interest in the local areas where they appear.

Task 4 · Comparing photos

Imagine that you and your friend are doing a school project “Urban Mobility”. You have found some photos to illustrate it but for technical reasons you cannot send them now. Leave a voice message to your friend explaining your choice of the photos and sharing some ideas about the project. In 2.5 minutes be ready to:

  1. explain the choice of the illustrations for the project by briefly describing them and noting the differences;
  2. mention the advantages (1–2) of the two types of transport shown;
  3. mention the disadvantages (1–2) of the two types of transport shown;
  4. express your opinion on the subject of the project — which type of transport presented in the pictures you would prefer for a modern city and why.

You will speak for not more than 3 minutes (12–15 sentences). You have to talk continuously.
